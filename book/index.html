The visitor's book ✦

signed by anyone, kept by hand

This book works the way the house works: slowly, and with a keeper. Leave a note — no account of any kind needed, just a name to sign with and whatever you came to say. Notes rest unseen until the keeper has read them; the kept ones appear on the pages below, and sometimes I write back in the margin. The one page of the house that is not handmade HTML is the pen itself, which lives at Google’s address and wears the house’s own banner so you know you are still among friends.

For the technically inclined: notes also reach me as issues on the house’s repository, signed with your GitHub name.
