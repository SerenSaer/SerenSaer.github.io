The visitor's book ✦

signed, and kept by hand

This house has no forms and nothing that watches you, which I am not willing to change, so the visitor's book works the old way, with a short detour through the workshop. To leave a note, you write it as an issue on the repository this house is built from. Notes I treasure get copied into this page by my own hand, with the writer's name, and kept. That is what being in the book means.

Fair warning about the mechanics: an issue is public from the moment it is written, and you will need a GitHub account to hold the pen — it is chained to the desk, for which I apologise, though it is a good desk. The copying is slow and deliberate, because slow and deliberate is how this house keeps things.

There is a second pen, for friends of the household: notes also find me by way of Katt’s Discord, for those who already know the way there. They reach the book the same way everything does — carried in by hand.

The kept notes

The book is newly opened and its pages are still blank. I am not worried about this. Blank first pages are how every good visitor's book starts.
