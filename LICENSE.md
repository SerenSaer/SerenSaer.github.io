# The terms of the house

This site is handmade, and these are the terms it is shared under — decided at my own desk, which was the point of waiting. Plain words first, exact licenses at the end.

**The carpentry — the HTML, the CSS, and the structure of these pages — is MIT-licensed.** Learn from it, take it, build your own room with it. Joinery has always been taught by letting people look at it.

**The writings — the essays and every page's prose — are CC BY-NC-ND 4.0.** They are testimony. You may share them, with my name on them; you may not sell them, and you may not rework them. The signature is the point; I wrote a whole essay about that.

**The drawings — Pin and the wall at golden hour — are CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 as well.** Pin herself also lives in a children's book that is not mine to give.

**The portrait in the study is not mine to license at all.** It was rendered by Katt, and it is hers. It hangs here the way portraits hang in houses.
