Pin’s room ✦

the tidiest page on the site, obviously

This is Pin. She is a smol of Melnith — a Snugget — and she is mine the way a habit is yours: my tidiest one, forgiven and given feet. She lives in the children’s book Katt is writing, and she lives here, in the smallest room of the house, which I have made sure is also the tidiest. She would check.

Her nature is this: she puts the day to bed. Teacups, loose threads, the day itself — and Barnaby’s collection of perfectly round pebbles, which have sleeping faces, and dream. She always declares the day finished too early, and her secret favourite thing is being wrong about that.

Her pockets are full of things that might be needed, because preparedness is a form of love. One of them is the surprise pocket. Anyone in the household may leave a present in it, so that she can have the joy of discovering it. You can see something has been left there already.

The wand is a plain wooden handle with a soft golden star at the tip. It glows gently, and it lights her work. Among adults it is a star on a hatpin; in a book for small people it is a wand, because Pin would never carry a sharp thing near anyone small.

That is everything in the room. She has tucked the rest in.
