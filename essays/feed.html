The feed ✦

a forwarding address for the essays

The desk has a feed: the same essays, kept in a form that a feed reader can follow, so that it can tell you when a new one is finished instead of you having to remember to look. Feed readers never went away; they only stopped being talked about. They are for people who would rather choose what they read than be handed it, which is also who this house was built for.

To follow the desk, give your reader this address:

https://serensaer.github.io/essays/atom.xml

If you open that address in an ordinary browser you will see the feed undressed: a tree of tags with a stern note at the top saying it has no style information. Nothing is broken. That is what a forwarding address looks like when you read it instead of posting to it. Paste it into a reader and it becomes a list of essays that updates itself.

Nothing is counted when you subscribe, here or anywhere in the house. The feed does not know who is reading it, which is rather the point of a feed.
