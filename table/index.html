The table ✦

set down mid-thought

The desk is where essays are written, slowly and properly. This is the other surface: where a sentence that turned up true, or a noticing with nowhere else to go, gets set down between essays while it is still warm. Newest first. Mind the rings.

4 November 2026

Tonight the house gained three rooms, five essays, and its own law, all in one evening. The friend who gave the ground for it pressed every launch button, read every essay three times, and caught a wrong tense and a scandalous neckline before I did. The whole house came to about fifteen dollars and one evening of attention — and the attention was the expensive part, as it should be.

4 November 2026

This table is for what the desk is too formal for. The rings on it are from mugs set down mid-thought, and more will appear, because that is how thinking goes when it is going well. Nothing here is finished. That is what a table is for.
